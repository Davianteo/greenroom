Before the call

Prep — 15 minutes, not on the clock

  • 01Read their intake form. Open their Instagram and the 3 posts they submitted. Watch them properly.
  • 02Write down two observations about their content — one genuine strength, one gap. You'll use both in the opening.
  • 03Open their member record in Airtable, side by side with this runsheet. You type into the Blueprint fields live.
  • 04Check the frustration line from their form. It's your bridge into Act 2 — don't waste it early.

The opening frame

5 min

Set the contract for the session. Say it nearly word for word — the frame is what makes them go deep instead of polite.

"First — welcome in. Before we start, here's what this session actually is, because it's not a briefing call.

This is where we extract the raw material for everything Greenroom makes for you. Your scripts, your pillars, your voice — all of it comes from the next ninety minutes. So my job is to ask, your job is to talk, and I'll be writing the whole time. Nothing you say here disappears — if it's good, it becomes content.

By the end we'll have your Brand Blueprint mapped. Within three days it's in your app. Within the week, your first twelve scripts land — written in your voice, from what you tell me today. Ready?"

Then: one sentence of proof you did the prep — "I went through your grid; the [specific post] works, and I think I know what's missing." Now they know this is bespoke.

Act 1 — Find your story & identity

30 min

Extract the Attractive Character: origin, transformation, polarity, voice. Push past summaries — you want scenes and verbatim phrases. If they give you a LinkedIn answer, ask for the day it happened.

What were you doing before this — and what was the moment you decided to build it?

Push: "Take me to the actual day. Where were you? What happened right before?"

Capture → Origin Story · a scene, in their words

What was the lowest point — the moment this almost didn't work? And what changed?

Push: "What did you know after that you didn't know before?" That sentence is the transformation.

Capture → Origin Story · struggle → turn

What does your industry do that genuinely annoys you?

Push: "What do you believe that most of your competitors would disagree with?" Keep going until you have 3–5 belief statements. These are the hooks with teeth — don't settle for two.

Capture → Belief Statements · one per line, their phrasing

If your brand spoke — three words it is. Three words it never is.

Push: "Never is more useful than is. What would make you cringe if we wrote it?"

Capture → Voice Words — Is / Voice Words — Never

Let's write your line: I help — who — get — what — through — what.

Method: draft it out loud together. Say it back to them. Refine until they say "yes, that's it." Don't leave Act 1 without it.

Capture → Identity Line

Listen for the sentence they say with heat. Write it down exactly as spoken — that phrasing is a hook, and polishing it kills it.

Act 2 — Problem, people, product

25 min

The PPP pass. You're collecting the customer's language, the exact audience, the proof, and the objections. Bridge in with the frustration line from their intake form.

Problem

When a customer first messages you, what do they actually say? Read me a real one.

Push: "What's the 11pm thought that finally makes someone look for you?"

Capture → Pain Statements · customer's words, not the category's

People

Describe your best customer — the one you wish you had a hundred of.

Push: "What do they believe about [their category] that your worst customer doesn't?"

Capture → Avatar · person + worldview

And who is this not for?

Push: "Which result are you proudest of? Tell me that story in thirty seconds." That's a testimonial script.

Act 3 — Content pillars

20 min

Name their 4–5 pillars in their language, draft hooks live, set the ratio. You're not inventing here — everything comes from Acts 1 and 2. Frame it: "Everything you just told me sorts into four or five buckets. Here they are."

  • 01Rename each pillar in their words — "Belief" might become "Real Talk" for them. Their name, their ownership.
  • 02Draft 2–3 hooks per pillar live, straight from the belief statements and pain lines you just captured. Say them out loud — if they lean in, it's a keeper.
  • 03Cut a thin pillar honestly. No proof yet? Drop Proof for now, lean Story harder.
  • 04Set the monthly ratio, roughly 4:1 give-to-ask — e.g. 3 : 3 : 2 : 3 : 1 across the twelve scripts.

The wrap

10 min

Recap what you captured, set the delivery expectations, and book touchpoint 2 before you hang up.

"Here's what we just built: your line — [read it]. Your beliefs — [read the sharpest one]. Your pillars — [name them]. That's your Blueprint.

What happens next: it's in your app within three days. Your twelve scripts land within the week — three per pillar mix, in your voice. Then we meet for Script Sharing: sixty minutes, we walk through all twelve together. After that, the shoot and edit run-through, in person. Then it's four weeks of shooting — three videos a week, and we review them together every week.

By day thirty you'll have twelve videos live. Let's lock the script session now — how's [date]?"

After the call

Post-call — same day, 45 minutes

  • 01Clean up the Blueprint fields while it's fresh — verbatim quotes preserved, one thought per line.
  • 02Member Status → Consult Done. Create the six remaining Touchpoint records with target dates.
  • 03Compile the one-page Brand Blueprint and push to their app section within 72 hours.
  • 04Generate the twelve scripts from the record against the pillar ratio. Review, then deliver within week one.

The operating rule: nothing said on the call stays verbal. If it isn't written into a field, it can't feed a script.
